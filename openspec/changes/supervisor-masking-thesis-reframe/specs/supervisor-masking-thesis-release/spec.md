## Purpose

Defines the manuscript and submission release that communicates why rule-based supervision achieves nominal give-way behaviour yet limits identification and transfer of predictor and risk-allocation improvements.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Finding-led manuscript argument
The manuscript SHALL be organised around one bounded argument: in the tested Town05 give-way system, complete rule-based supervisor authority achieves nominal physical yielding, while upstream predictor and risk-allocation improvements do not uniformly transfer to executed behaviour and are only called masked when the evidence identifies that mechanism.

#### Scenario: Title and abstract are finalised
- **WHEN** Results and Discussion have passed the evidence audit
- **THEN** the title and abstract lead with the supervisor effectiveness--limitation finding, include quantitative support, and avoid universal safety or unsupported causal masking claims

#### Scenario: Reader encounters the hypotheses
- **WHEN** the Introduction is read
- **THEN** H1, H2 with Capacity--Information--Architecture subquestions, and H3 are explicit, falsifiable and connected to one downstream transfer question

### Requirement: Reproducible system explanation
The Problem Formulation and Methodology SHALL define the right-hand-traffic ego-left-turn versus opposing-straight give-way geometry and explain MultiPath, multimodal SMPC, risk allocation and the rule-based supervisor from inputs to outputs with consistent notation.

#### Scenario: MultiPath is explained
- **WHEN** a reader follows the prediction subsection
- **THEN** raster/state inputs, trajectory anchors or modes, mixture probabilities, means, covariance parameterisation, task adaptation, training loss and inference outputs are defined and linked to the tensors consumed by SMPC

#### Scenario: SMPC is explained
- **WHEN** a reader follows the controller subsection
- **THEN** ego dynamics, multimodal target uncertainty, horizon and clocks, normalized joint-mode probability-weighted expected branch cost, shared penalties, branch-specific control, collision approximation, chance-constraint tightening, solver output and fallback meaning are defined and each symbol is used consistently

#### Scenario: Risk allocation is explained
- **WHEN** fixed and adaptive risk policies are introduced
- **THEN** the probability budget, per-mode allocation or tightening, feasibility constraints and the way allocation changes collision constraints are stated mathematically and operationally

#### Scenario: Supervisor is explained
- **WHEN** the supervisor subsection is read
- **THEN** all implemented behavioural authority channels, trigger inputs, candidate-to-executed command mapping, bypass/recovery behaviour and logged pre/post measurement points are defined without presenting the bundle as a certified minimal safety filter

### Requirement: Formula-to-code traceability
Every principal equation SHALL be followed by a plain-language execution explanation and a source or code locator; detailed derivations and complete parameter tables SHALL be placed in appendices when they would interrupt the main argument.

#### Scenario: Equation audit runs
- **WHEN** the manuscript is prepared for release
- **THEN** every symbol is defined before or at first use, units and time indices are consistent, no equation is orphaned from the algorithm, and implementation-specific deviations from the reference SMPC paper are explicit

### Requirement: Evidence-led Results and Discussion
Results SHALL report observations before interpretation, and Discussion SHALL evaluate masking, rival explanations and limitations at the causal strength licensed by the evidence.

#### Scenario: H2 is reported
- **WHEN** the predictor results are written
- **THEN** offline Capacity--Information--Architecture findings, deployed predictor comparison and downstream physical contrast are separated, with missing cross-layer cells made explicit rather than silently inferred

#### Scenario: H3 is reported
- **WHEN** risk-allocation results are written
- **THEN** the fixed frontier, adaptive contexts, constraint/solver evidence and executed outcomes are shown together without outcome-selected comparators

#### Scenario: Rival explanations are discussed
- **WHEN** physical policies look similar
- **THEN** supervisor compression, inactive constraints, solver fallback, controller insensitivity, target behaviour and limited statistical power are considered before assigning a mechanism

### Requirement: Academic figures and tables
All result figures and the project architecture figure SHALL be generated by committed Python/Matplotlib code with explicit legends, axes, units, uncertainty and provenance; styling SHALL be restrained and suitable for an academic manuscript.

#### Scenario: Cross-layer figure is generated
- **WHEN** the system figure is rendered
- **THEN** it shows the exact give-way geometry and the measured MultiPath-to-risk-to-SMPC-to-supervisor-to-trajectory path rather than a generic autonomous-driving stack

#### Scenario: Masking figures are generated
- **WHEN** H2 and H3 figures are rendered
- **THEN** each figure contains multiple comparable series or panels spanning upstream, candidate and physical layers, while visually separating incompatible populations

### Requirement: Submission release audit
The final release SHALL include manuscript source, bibliography, compiled PDF, generated figure source and assets, evidence tables, build instructions, hypothesis verdicts, bounded limitations and a manifest linking experiment and dissertation commits.

#### Scenario: Final audit passes
- **WHEN** the dissertation is ready to submit
- **THEN** required section order, citations, numerical claims, equation symbols, figure provenance, PDF rendering, tests, clean worktrees, Git object integrity and remote divergence all pass with no unresolved submission dependency
