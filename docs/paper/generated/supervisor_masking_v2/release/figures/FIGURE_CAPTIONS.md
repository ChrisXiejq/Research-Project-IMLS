# Supervisor-masking thesis figure captions

## Figure 1. Right-hand-traffic give-way task and cross-layer operator chain

The ego turns left across an opposing priority vehicle whose motion line defines a dynamic route conflict point. MultiPath supplies mode probabilities, means and per-step covariances; risk allocation parameterises the multimodal SMPC; the complete rule bundle can intervene through seven audited channels before and after optimisation. Measurements are attached to their identified layer.

## Figure 2. Complete supervisor authority is active and decisive in the tested task

SF4 toggles the complete seven-channel bundle across 10 initialization groups. Authority enabled completed 40/40 rollouts with no yield failure or adverse collision; monitor only completed 0/40 and is outcome-floor saturated. Requests remained observable when actuation was blocked, while command replacement and rule bypass occurred only with authority enabled. These are nominal tested-sample outcomes, not a formal safety guarantee or a channel-specific causal effect.

## Figure 3. Predictor distinctions are retained upstream but do not transfer uniformly

Foundation adaptation (F1), the Capacity–Information–Architecture study (F4), and deployed closed-loop transfer (F5) are visually separated and not pooled. P* retains an in-loop ADE advantage under fixed risk, while completion and clearance contrasts show no uniform physical advantage. CIA candidates were not all deployed, and the factual trajectories are not same-state counterfactuals; the result is consistent with, but does not identify, supervisor-specific masking.

## Figure 4. Risk allocation is active, context dependent and progressively compressed

All 12 declared adaptive-versus-fixed frontier comparisons are retained in R3 (F2); adaptive dominates three. In V3 (F5), adaptive allocation changes tightening by about 0.26 while nominal and executed mean-acceleration contrasts are much smaller. SF4 (F3) supplies an authority interaction, but the monitor-only arm completes 0/40 and is floor saturated. Populations are juxtaposed, not pooled; these factual trajectories do not identify supervisor-specific attenuation.
