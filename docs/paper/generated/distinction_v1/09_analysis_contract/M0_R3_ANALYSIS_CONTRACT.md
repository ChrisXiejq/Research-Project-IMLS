# M0: frozen R3 analysis contract

This contract was written before any R3 formal outcome was generated or read. The independent sample size is five init groups, not 80 rollouts and not simulator steps.

H3 asks a deliberately strong question: does B1’s offline gain translate consistently in every policy/style setting? B1 must be no slower on fixed-geometry adjusted delay and no worse on footprint separation in all eight cells, without extra collision, yield or completion failures. A mixed pattern does not support universal transfer.

H4 tests empirical Pareto dominance, not whether adaptive risk is “usually good”. Adaptive must be no slower and no less separated than every fixed comparator, with at least one strict improvement and no extra failure, in every predictor/style stratum. A safety–efficiency trade-off is useful evidence but is not dominance.

All five paired init effects will be shown. Exact sign-flip tests and Holm-adjusted values will be reported, but with five clusters the smallest non-zero two-sided exact p-value is 0.0625. Bootstrap intervals are descriptive. Legacy rollouts and the R2 pilot cannot be pooled into R3.
