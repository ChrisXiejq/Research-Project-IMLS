\section{Problem formulation}
\label{sec:problem}

\subsection{Coupled prediction--planning system}

At time $t$, the predictor $\model$ consumes a raster observation $r_t$ and,
for sequence models, an interaction history $s_{t-H:t}$ with validity mask
$m_{t-H:t}$. It outputs a $K$-component trajectory distribution
\begin{equation}
  p_{\theta}(Y_{t+1:t+T}\mid r_t,s_{t-H:t},m_{t-H:t})
  = \sum_{k=1}^{K}\pi_k\,
  \mathcal{N}(Y;\mu_k,\Sigma_k).
\end{equation}
A risk policy maps this distribution and the current state to a risk budget
$\risk_t$. A trajectory solver then constructs a candidate ego trajectory;
the supervisor may override it before execution. Figure~\ref{fig:chain}
summarises this implemented feedback path.

\subsection{Offline objectives}

The primary selection metric is rollout-macro mixture NLL per valid prediction
step. Secondary metrics are top-1 ADE and FDE. Macro aggregation first averages
valid windows within a rollout and then averages rollouts, avoiding the
incorrect treatment of overlapping windows as independent experiments.

\subsection{Closed-loop objectives}

Closed-loop performance is multi-objective. The corrected primary efficiency
outcome is event-clock completion time. The primary continuous safety-margin
outcome is minimum vehicle-footprint separation computed from actual actor
bounding boxes. Native/footprint collision, fixed-geometry yield failure and
completion failure are binary guards. Solver failure and supervisor activity
are mechanism measurements, not interchangeable safety outcomes. A policy
dominates another only if completion and separation are no worse and no excess
binary scientific failure occurs within the declared comparison scope.

\subsection{Scope of inference}

The target population is the controlled Town05 give-way geometry under the
specified routes and target styles. It excludes other maps, junctions,
weather, traffic populations and real vehicles. Corrected R3 uses five newly
generated paired initialisation groups (101--105) as the independent clusters
for uncertainty calculations.
