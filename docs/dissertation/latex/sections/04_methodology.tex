\section{Methodology}
\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{CARLA scenario and system architecture}

Experiments use CARLA 0.9.14 in synchronous Town05 at 20~Hz with ClearNoon
weather \citep{dosovitskiy2017carla}. A Mercedes-Benz coupe ego follows the
left-turn route from intersection node 0 to node 3 at a nominal 6~m/s. A
matching target coupe travels straight from node 2 to node 2 at 9~m/s and is
either assertive or reactive. Each rollout is capped at 600 simulator steps
(30~s). In the A3 risk-owned-yield authority regime, fixed and adaptive
policies share the corrected three-mode SMPC, $-3$~m/s$^2$ reference
acceleration lower bound and safety supervisor. The ego optimisation horizon
is ten 0.2-s steps. The route-defined conflict radius is 4.0~m, while the
primary post-CARLA footprint replay adds 0.25~m per actor using the actual
CARLA bounding boxes. Table~\ref{tab:risk-contract} records the risk settings;
the full yield-state thresholds remain hash-bound in the frozen tuning file.

\subsection{Data collection and representation}

The factorial collection contains 200 rollouts: 50 ego initialisations crossed
with two target styles and two collection policies. Each prediction sample
contains a deployment-equivalent raster and six interaction tokens covering
$-1.0$ to $0.0$~s. Each token has 12 relative position, velocity, yaw and
distance features. The target future covers ten steps at 0.2~s intervals, for
a 2.0~s horizon, in the current target-local right-handed frame. Target style,
policy, factorial cell, split and initialisation ID are prohibited inputs.

Rollouts, not windows, are split: initialisations 1--40 train, 41--45
validation and 46--50 test. Normalisation is fit on train only. Samples with no
future label are excluded; partial-horizon samples use a masked loss.

\subsection{Prediction models}

\Bzero{} is the frozen pretrained MultiPath control. \Bone{} retrains only the
final MultiPath dense prediction head. \BtwoM{} and \BtwoD{} add flattened-
sequence MLP adapters; the former updates trajectory means and the latter
updates means, covariance/orientation and mixture logits. \Tone{} and \Ttwo{}
replace the corresponding MLP adapter with a masked Transformer. Within each
residual-output scope the MLP/Transformer adapter sizes are approximately
matched, but the full comparison is not parameter matched to \Bone{}:
\Bone{} has approximately 1.034 million trainable parameters, whereas the
adapter variants have approximately 77--176 thousand. H2 therefore compares
frozen training configurations, not architecture in isolation.

Table~\ref{tab:model-training} gives the frozen training contract. All models
use 16 MultiPath anchors and ten future steps. The masked loss assigns the
nearest valid anchor, combines mixture classification with bivariate Gaussian
regression, and averages only over valid future steps. B1 freezes every layer
except the final dense prediction head. The residual adapters freeze the base;
B2 variants flatten the six 12-feature tokens and validity mask into two
80-unit GELU layers, while T variants use one width-64 Transformer block with
four heads, a 128-unit feed-forward layer, masked mean pooling and dropout
0.1. Mean-only variants update trajectory means; distributional variants also
update scale, orientation and mixture logits.

\subsection{Training, selection and calibration}

Five candidate variants are trained with seeds 11, 23 and 37 under the same
split, anchors, decoder, loss, optimiser budget and validation ranking metric.
The variant is selected by median validation rollout-macro NLL and a
representative seed is chosen using validation only. Selection is frozen before
the test set is opened. Context zero/shuffle ablations and collision-filtered
sensitivity runs are post-selection diagnostics and do not alter the primary
model selection.

Calibration parameters are fit on validation and frozen for test/deployment.
The closed-loop contrast uses the identity-calibrated \Bzero{} stack and the
validation-calibrated \Bone{} stack; it must therefore be interpreted as a
frozen predictor-stack treatment rather than a weights-only intervention.

\subsection{Statistical analysis}

Effect estimates can average the balanced paired conditions, but uncertainty
and exact sign-flip tests operate on five ego-initialisation cluster means.
The smallest attainable non-zero two-sided exact p-value is therefore 0.0625.
Holm correction is applied within each predefined family. Results emphasise
effect sizes, cluster intervals, sign consistency and exact/Holm p-values;
windows and simulator steps are never treated as independent replications.

\subsection{Data-quality audit and sensitivity}

Logging recorded 253 collision callbacks in six reactive training rollouts.
Episode reconstruction reduced these to 16 contact episodes and 62 unique
frames involving the target and infrastructure, not 253 ego--target crashes.
Because old callbacks cannot be aligned exactly to the sample clock, all 162
usable windows from the six rollouts are conservatively removed in a
validation-only sensitivity analysis. The primary dataset remains frozen; the
sensitivity asks only whether the architecture ranking changes.
