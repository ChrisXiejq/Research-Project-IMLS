\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

This section reports observations in the order of the research questions. It
does not explain mechanisms beyond what the measured diagnostics support.

\subsection{Dataset integrity and coverage}

All 200 planned collection rollouts completed. Day6 and merged Day7 manifests
agree on 11,230 raw samples; rollout groups are disjoint across splits and all
normalisation/leakage gates pass. Table~\ref{tab:dataset} gives the usable

\subsection{Offline selection favours simple task adaptation}

Across three validation seeds, median macro NLL ranks \Bone{} (1.86055),
\BtwoD{} (1.87274), \Ttwo{} (1.87789), \Tone{} (2.00883) and \BtwoM{}
(2.02553). The frozen-test order is consistent. Relative to pretrained
\Bzero{}, \Bone{} reduces test top-1 ADE from 1.299 to 0.106~m, FDE from 2.685
to 0.129~m and uncalibrated macro NLL from 2.171 to 1.857~nats/step. Thus H1 is
supported in distribution, while H3 is refuted for the architectures and
budget tested.

\subsection{Sequence-use evidence and calibration-tail failure}

Shuffling the interaction sequence worsens \Tone{} and \Ttwo{} NLL by 0.0848
and 0.1494; zeroing \Ttwo{} worsens NLL by 0.0908. H2 is therefore supported as
an input-sensitivity mechanism, but the result is not evidence of causal
understanding. For \Bone{}, frozen global calibration improves aggregate NLL
from 1.8571 to $-2.0686$, while worsening the response-active subset from
2.0763 to 8.5728. Negative continuous-density NLL is valid. The tail diagnostic
contains only 15 windows from six rollouts and three init groups.

\subsection{Nominal safety--efficiency frontier}

All 80 Day10 rollouts completed, with zero observed footprint collision,
yield-order failure or completion failure. These zero counts are reliability
observations, not estimates of zero population risk. No predictor--risk
combination uniformly occupies the preferred high-separation/low-delay region.
For reactive targets with \Bone{}, adaptive risk has 8.59~s delay and 1.200~m
margin, versus 8.34~s and 1.186~m for fixed aggressive: a trade-off rather than
dominance. H5 is refuted.

  \caption{Nominal closed-loop safety--efficiency frontier. Each mark is a
  five-rollout cell mean; no policy uniformly dominates across target styles.}
  \label{fig:frontier}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Predictor effects depend on policy and arrival timing}

The 120-rollout synthesis does not support a uniform transfer from offline
accuracy to closed-loop benefit. Pooled across offsets, \Bone{} minus \Bzero{}
adjusted delay is $-0.370$~s under fixed medium (cluster interval
$[-0.717,-0.023]$) and $-0.337$~s under adaptive risk
($[-0.665,0.015]$), but Holm-adjusted exact tests do not provide confirmatory
significance. Under fixed medium the effect changes direction, from
$-0.755$~s at $-3$~m to $+0.295$~s at 0~m. H4 is refuted and H6 receives only
descriptive support.

\begin{figure}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.98\linewidth]{figure06_predictor_effect_moderation.png}
  \caption{Predictor-effect moderation. \Bone{}--\Bzero{} effects and
  five-init cluster intervals vary with risk policy and arrival offset.}
  \label{fig:moderation}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Timing changes multiple downstream mechanisms}

For \Bone{}, shifting arrival from $-3$ to $+3$~m increases footprint
separation by 0.519~m under fixed medium and 0.896~m under adaptive risk,
increases solver failure by 2.05 and 2.38 percentage points, and decreases
supervisor activity by 7.07 and 6.94 percentage points. The mechanisms move
simultaneously, so the supervisor cannot be identified as the single cause of
the outcome.

  \caption{Arrival-timing mechanisms. Separation, solver feasibility and
  supervisor intervention change together as target timing is shifted.}
  \label{fig:timing}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Sensitivity and hypothesis synthesis}

Removing the conservative upper bound of 162 potentially affected training
windows and repeating all 15 validation runs leaves the architecture order
unchanged; \Bone{} median NLL changes by only $+0.00132$. H7 is refuted and the
original experiment remains primary. All frozen deployment reliability gates
pass, supporting H8 only for the observed runs.
