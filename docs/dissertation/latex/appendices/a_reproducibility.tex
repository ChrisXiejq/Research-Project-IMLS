\subsection{Model and optimisation hyperparameters}

All trainable variants use 16 fixed MultiPath anchors, a ten-step 2-s future,
the rollout-disjoint split in Table~\ref{tab:dataset}, batch size 16 and shuffle
buffer 1,024. Adam uses learning rate $10^{-4}$ and gradient clip norm 10. The
maximum is 20 epochs with patience 5 on validation loss. B1 exposes only the
final dense prediction head (1,034,208 trainable parameters). B2-M and T1
update means only (77,600 and 86,688 parameters); B2-D and T2 update means,
scale/orientation and mixture logits (176,096 and 165,728 parameters). MLPs use
two width-80 GELU layers and dropout 0.1. Transformers use one width-64 block,
four attention heads, width-128 feed-forward layer, masked mean pooling and
dropout 0.1. The complete 15-run table is generated in
Table~\ref{tab:app-validation}.

The masked MultiPath loss assigns the closest anchor over valid target steps,
then combines mixture classification with bivariate Gaussian regression and
normalises only by the valid-step count. Architecture ranking uses median
validation rollout-macro uncalibrated trajectory NLL per step. Calibration is
fitted after training on validation, reported for every run, and excluded from
architecture ranking. No retraining or retuning is permitted after the frozen
test is opened.

Fixed Gaussian tightenings are 1.2816, 1.6400 and 2.0537. The adaptive rule
uses approach/critical/near pre-clearance floors 1.66/1.72/1.78; every arm
shares three modes, $a_{\min}=-3$~m/s$^2$, the same route/controller and a
ten-step planning horizon. Rollouts stop at valid route completion or the
600-step/30-s cap. The primary completion clock is
\texttt{smpc\_completion.step}/\texttt{carla\_fps}; it includes the simulator
step that sets \texttt{goal\_reached}. Minimum footprint separation is the
minimum oriented-rectangle distance from the actual CARLA actor bounding boxes
with 0.25~m inflation per actor. Frozen sensitivity margins are 0, 0.25, 0.35
and 0.50~m; 0.25~m is always primary.

Infrastructure failures may be retried up to ten isolated attempts with
$5k$-second backoff after failed attempt $k$. Allowed classes are spawn/setup,
CARLA connection or timeout, process-resource, external-interruption and world-
hygiene failures. Completed rollouts and scientific outcomes are never retried
or excluded; unknown classes stop automation. Thus retry logic cannot select a
favourable scientific trajectory.

\subsection{Statistical algorithms}

Prediction NLL is first averaged across valid future steps and then within each
rollout; rollouts, not windows, form the macro-average. For R3, every contrast
is calculated within init group and the five paired effects are the independent
units. The reported effect is their arithmetic mean. A descriptive 95\%
percentile cluster-bootstrap interval resamples the five effects with
replacement 20,000 times. Its global seed is 20260808; each contrast seed is
the integer encoded by the first 16 hexadecimal characters of
SHA-256(\texttt{global-seed|contrast-id|metric}).

The exact test enumerates all $2^n$ sign assignments for the $n$ complete
paired effects and reports the two-sided fraction whose absolute mean is at
least the observed absolute mean. H3 has two eight-contrast Holm families, one
per primary outcome. H4 has one three-comparator Holm family per
predictor--style--outcome stratum. Missing continuous pairs are listed and are
not imputed; unresolved binary guards block universal support. Timing and
distance dominance tolerances (0.05~s and $10^{-6}$~m) represent sampling and
floating-point resolution, not equivalence margins.

\subsection{Executable audit trail}

The formal collection entry point is
\texttt{core/scripts/carla/run\_r3\_corrected\_formal\_matrix.sh}; the analysis
entry point is
\texttt{core/scripts/models/analyze\_r3\_corrected\_formal.py}. W1 presentation
tables are regenerated with
\texttt{python3 core/scripts/models/build\_w1\_latex\_evidence.py}; the two R3
PNGs are deterministic format conversions by
\texttt{render\_w1\_r3\_figures\_png.cjs}. Finally, \texttt{make pdf} in
\texttt{docs/dissertation/latex} builds the TMLR-layout manuscript. These paths
are audit identifiers; the submitted evidence package, rather than this prose,
is authoritative for hashes and values.
