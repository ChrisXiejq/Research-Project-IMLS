\subsection{Primary and secondary evidence boundary}

Primary evidence comprises the rollout-disjoint prediction protocol, frozen
offline test and corrected 80-rollout R3 matrix. Physical baselines,
input/capacity audits and in-loop manipulation checks support interpretation.
H3's B1/B0 estimand is the validation-calibrated deployed-stack contrast, not
a weight-only effect.
Legacy Day10--13 timing, calibration-tail, collision reconstruction and
filtered validation analyses are diagnostic or sensitivity evidence. They do
not reopen model selection and are never pooled with corrected R3.

\subsection{Result provenance}

Every headline numerical sentence in the W1 source has an adjacent
comment containing an M1 evidence identifier, for example:

\begin{verbatim}
% EVIDENCE: H1_B1_MINUS_B0_TEST_NLL
% SOURCE: docs/paper/generated/distinction_v1/
%         10_four_hypothesis_evidence/M1_EVIDENCE_MANIFEST.json
\end{verbatim}

The submitted archive should include the LaTeX source, bibliography, official
style assets, generated figures and the machine-readable evidence package,
while excluding secrets, credentials and non-anonymised metadata.
