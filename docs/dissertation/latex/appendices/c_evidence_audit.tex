\subsection{Primary and secondary evidence boundary}

Primary evidence comprises the rollout-disjoint collection, three-seed
validation selection, frozen test, and the nominal/shifted formal closed-loop
matrices. Context ablations, calibration subsets, collision reconstruction and
Day13 filtered validation are diagnostic or sensitivity evidence. They support
mechanism and robustness claims but do not reopen model selection.

\subsection{Result provenance}

Every numerical sentence in the final paper should have an adjacent source
comment of the form:

\begin{verbatim}
% EVIDENCE: R_TEST_B1_MINUS_B0_ADE
% SOURCE: docs/paper/generated/paper_assets_v1/paper_key_results.csv
\end{verbatim}

The submitted archive should include the LaTeX source, bibliography, official
style assets, figures and a machine-readable claim-to-evidence table, while
excluding secrets, raw credentials and non-anonymised supplementary metadata.
